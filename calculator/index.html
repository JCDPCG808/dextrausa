What the listing covers

Durabar #3 carries ICC-ES ESR-5501 under AC521 — temperature and shrinkage reinforcement in slabs-on-grade, plain footings and flatwork, at 12 in o.c. or tighter. It is not a structural listing. For beams, columns, walls and elevated slabs, Astec Plus carries ESR-5751 under AC454 at #4 and #8 — that is the structural path. Your engineer of record signs the layout, not this page.
