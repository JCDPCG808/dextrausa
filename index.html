Two listed paths

Durabar for the slab. Astec Plus when the member is structural.

Contractors

Durabar · ESR-5501

Temperature-and-shrinkage reinforcement for slabs-on-grade, plain footings and flatwork. Listed #3 at 12 in o.c. or tighter. Carpet rolls out; straight bar is also in stock.

  • Listed bond 3.55 ksi under ESR-5501
  • #4 Durabar is stocked for quoting — not an ESR-5501 listed size
  • ~4× lighter than Grade 60 steel by weight

SE / agency

Astec Plus · ESR-5751

Structural path under AC454. Listed #4 and #8. This is the bar for HDOT, Navy, precast and engineered members. EOR signs the layout.

  • #4 listed ultimate 40.72 kip · E 9,831 ksi
  • #8 listed ultimate 148.16 kip · E 9,394 ksi
  • 90° L-bends in Honolulu stock — factory only, no field bending

What we can put on an order today

Order book — listed sizes

We take orders on listed sizes for engineered jobs. Send the member, the size and the spec section, and we will confirm against the listing before we quote.

Durabar / Durabar Carpet

The listed slab-on-grade path. ESR-5501 under AC521 covers #3 temperature-and-shrinkage reinforcement at 12 in o.c. or tighter, in slabs-on-grade, plain footings and flatwork. We also stock #4 Durabar.

Astec Plus

The structural path. ESR-5751 under AC454 lists #4 and #8, which is what an engineered member needs.

E is stiffness. Steel is about 29,000 ksi — much stiffer than GFRP. That is expected and is why layouts differ.

Steel figures are Grade 60 yield. Durabar and Astec Plus figures are ICC-ES listed ultimate. Not the same limit state.

Fair turn on the comparison

Grade 60 steel is what crews buy today. Durabar covers listed #3 temperature-and-shrinkage at 12 in o.c. max. When the member needs listed #4 or #8, specify Astec Plus.
